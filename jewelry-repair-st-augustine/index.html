Bring it in and we'll take a look together

Giia Jeweler is a family-owned jewelry store and repair shop at 4213 US-1 in St. Augustine. Repairs are handled in-house by our jeweler, who has more than 20 years of experience in the jewelry industry. Bring your piece to the counter and we'll look at it with you and explain what it needs before any work begins.

Not sure whether something can be saved? Bring it in anyway. It costs nothing to have us look at it and tell you honestly what we can and can't do.

Repairs customers ask us about

  • Rings that need attention after years of wear
  • Broken chains and clasps
  • Loose or missing stones
  • Earrings, pendants, and bracelets
  • Gold and white gold pieces

We also handle watch repair and watch battery replacement, make custom jewelry, and buy gold, including broken pieces that aren't worth repairing.

Where to find us

We're on US-1 in St. Augustine, an easy drive from downtown, St. Augustine Beach, and the rest of St. Johns County. Customers from Palm Coast and Flagler County come up US-1 to us as well.

Jewelry Repair FAQ

Do you repair watches as well as jewelry?

Yes. We offer watch repair and battery replacement alongside jewelry repair.

What if my piece can't be repaired?

We'll tell you straight. If a piece isn't worth repairing and it's gold, we can talk about buying it instead.
