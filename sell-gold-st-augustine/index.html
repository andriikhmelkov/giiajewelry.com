Sell to a local jeweler, not a pop-up

Giia Jeweler buys gold at 4213 US-1 in St. Augustine. We're a family-owned store that's part of this town, not a traveling buyer working out of a hotel conference room for the weekend.

Bring in what you have and we'll go through it with you at the counter and tell you what we can offer. There's no obligation: if you'd rather keep it, keep it.

What to bring

  • Gold jewelry you no longer wear
  • Broken chains and damaged pieces
  • Odd single earrings

If a piece is worth more mended than melted, we'll say so. We also do jewelry repair and make custom pieces using stones and gold you already own.
