A short drive up US-1

To be clear about it: Giia Jeweler isn't a Palm Coast storefront. We're the family-owned jewelry store and repair shop at 4213 US-1 in St. Augustine, straight up US-1 from Palm Coast. Plenty of our customers come from Palm Coast, Flagler Beach, and the rest of Flagler County, because the drive is simple and they'd rather hand their jewelry to one experienced jeweler, with over 30 years in the trade, than to a mall counter.

Stay on US-1 heading north and it brings you right to us. If you'd rather have the route on your phone, use the directions link at the bottom of this page.

What you can get done in one trip

  • Jewelry repair, looked at with you at the counter before any work starts
  • Watch repair and battery replacement
  • Custom-made jewelry
  • Selling gold, including old and broken pieces

Call ahead on (904) 217-8647 if you want to know whether something is worth the trip. We'd rather tell you over the phone than have you drive up for nothing.
